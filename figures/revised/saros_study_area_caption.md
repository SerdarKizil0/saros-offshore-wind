**Figure 2.** Saros offshore-wind candidate zone and the environmental and bathymetric context used for screening. The candidate-zone boundary (black) is the official polygon published by the Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources in the candidate-YEKA notice of 13 May 2026 (file 'SAROS DÜRES'); it is a candidate area, not an approved project, and no turbine layout, cable route or installation footprint is implied. The zone covers 172.6 km² (WGS 84 / UTM zone 35N); its geometric centroid (open circle) lies at 40.557° N, 26.198° E. The energy model uses the ERA5 reanalysis grid node at 40.50° N, 26.25° E (black square), which lies outside the zone, 3.7 km from its nearest boundary (dotted line). The dashed vermillion line is the boundary of the Saros Körfezi Special Environmental Protection Area (ÖÇK) reconstructed from the coordinates in the annex to Council of Ministers Decision 2010/1089 (Official Gazette 27793, 22 December 2010). The annex does not state a geodetic datum; the coordinates were read as WGS 84, so the overlay is an approximate GIS reconstruction, not a legally certified boundary. The ÖÇK continues east and south of the map. About 25 km² (≈14%) of the zone, its eastern arm, falls inside the ÖÇK (hatched; 24.7 km² or 14.4% in the raster screening, 25.0 km² or 14.5% by exact polygon intersection). ÖÇK status requires permission from the environment ministry for activities; it is not an automatic prohibition of construction. Shading shows water depth below lowest astronomical tide (depth = −elevation) from the EMODnet Digital Bathymetry DTM 2024 (1/16 arc-minute grid) on a square-root colour scale. Depth inside the zone ranges from 1 to 62 m (median 28 m). The 10 m and 50 m isobaths mark the depth screening classes used in the site checks (shallower than 10 m; 50 m and deeper); they are screening thresholds, not legal or technical exclusion zones. Land is shown where the DTM elevation is 0 m or higher; islets and enclosed water bodies smaller than about 0.1 km² are omitted for legibility. The map extent is that of the bathymetry grid; only the tip of the Gelibolu Peninsula appears, in the south-eastern corner. The arrow points to true north; the scale bar is exact to within 0.1% across the map.
