# Long-term reanalysis-based energy yield of the Gulf of Saros offshore wind candidate area, Türkiye: sensitivity to reference period, grid node and wind data source

## Abstract

Türkiye published four candidate areas for offshore wind in May 2026. For the Gulf of Saros area, this study asks what a public reanalysis indicates
about the long-term energy yield, and how sensitive the answer is to choices
that are often left implicit. Hourly ERA5 wind speeds for 1994–2024 at the
grid node nearest the candidate polygon were extrapolated to a hub height of
105 m and converted hour by hour with a reference 9.5 MW power curve and
assumed losses. The net specific yield is about
{{energy_yield:net.gwh_per_mw|.2f}} GWh MW⁻¹ yr⁻¹, at a net capacity factor
of about {{energy_yield:net_cf|pct.0f}} %. The 90 % sampling confidence
interval of the 31-year mean is ±{{interannual_and_sampling:sampling_ci_of_period_mean.primary_t_interval_90.half_width_rel_pct|.1f}} %. The two neighbouring ERA5 output
nodes give {{node_sensitivity:nodes.W.energy_rel_to_primary_node_pct|+.1f}} %
and {{node_sensitivity:nodes.E.energy_rel_to_primary_node_pct|+.1f}} %;
these differences recur in every year and are several times larger than
that interval. The node used lies outside the polygon, at a point of strong land influence in the model. The Global Wind
Atlas indicates a mean wind speed about
{{d:gwa_polygon_excess_matched_pct|.0f}} % higher than ERA5 over the
atlas's mesoscale simulation period. Model data cannot resolve this disagreement, because the atlas is driven by ERA5. The most recent
11 years alone give a yield {{period_sensitivity:periods.2014-2024.energy_change_vs_reference_pct|abs.1f}} % higher than the 31-year mean. This
observed difference is of the same size as the sampling uncertainty of an
11-year mean (±{{d:windows.last_t90_half_width_pct|.1f}} %) and does not by itself establish a lasting change. The categories differ in kind and are
reported separately; they are not combined into one uncertainty interval. A
first-order screening shows water depths below
{{site_screening:bathymetry.depth_m.max|.0f}} m throughout the polygon and
an overlap of about
{{site_screening:protected_area_overlap.saros_ock_2010.overlap_share_pct|.0f}} %
with a Special Environmental Protection Area. No result is validated against
offshore measurements. For narrow coastal areas, the reference period, the grid node and the wind product should be reported and
tested, and measurements inside the polygon are the step that would most
improve knowledge of the resource.
