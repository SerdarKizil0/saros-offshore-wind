To the Editor-in-Chief, *Sustainable Energy Technologies and Assessments*

Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript "Long-term reanalysis-based energy yield of the Gulf
of Saros offshore wind candidate area, Türkiye: sensitivity to reference
period, grid node and wind data source" for consideration as a research
article.

In May 2026 Türkiye published four candidate areas for offshore wind,
including one in the Gulf of Saros. The manuscript asks what a public
reanalysis can say about the long-term energy yield of that candidate area
before any measurement exists, and how strongly the answer depends on three
choices that are often left implicit: the reference period, the reanalysis
grid node and the wind data product. It estimates the net specific yield
from 31 years (1994–2024) of hourly ERA5 data with a reference turbine and
reports the sampling confidence interval of the mean separately from the
sensitivity to neighbouring grid nodes, to the Global Wind Atlas and to the
reference period and the loss assumptions. These categories are kept apart
rather than merged into one interval. The polygon is also screened for water
depth and for overlap with a Special Environmental Protection Area.

The Gulf of Saros has been examined before in a techno-economic feasibility
assessment based on the Global Wind Atlas (Yildirim, 2023). The present work
does not repeat such an assessment; it addresses the sensitivity and
representativeness of a reanalysis-based yield estimate for the officially
published 2026 polygon. All results are model-based and are not validated
against offshore measurements, and the manuscript states this throughout.
The analysis is fully scripted and reproducible from checksum-pinned inputs.
